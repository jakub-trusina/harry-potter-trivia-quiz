    How to Play

    Getting Started

    Enter your wizard name and join the game. Wait for 2-4 players to join before starting.

    Game Rules

    • Each player starts with a capitol territory and must expand their influence
    • Attack adjacent territories by answering Harry Potter trivia questions
    • Territories must be connected to your capitol via supply lines to be used for attacks
    • Lose your capitol and you're eliminated!

    Territory Values

    • Each territory has a value (1-3)
    • Higher value territories are harder to capture but worth more points
    • Your score is the sum of all your territory values

    Combat System

    • When attacking, you'll face a Harry Potter trivia question
    • Answer correctly to capture the territory
    • Answer incorrectly and the attack fails
    • For capitol attacks, you need to win 3 rounds to capture it

    Winning the Game

    • Be the last player with a capitol to win
    • Or have the highest score when the game ends
